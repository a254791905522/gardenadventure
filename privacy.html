According to our App Store privacy details, we do not collect data from this app.

That matches Apple’s “Data Not Collected” label for Seed Grow Garden Adventure: the developer does not collect data from the app in the categories Apple asks developers to report.

What this means for you

Seed Grow Garden Adventure is designed as a straightforward puzzle experience. We do not use the app to gather personal information for our own analytics, advertising profiles, or similar purposes as described in that store disclosure.

  • No account is required inside the app for the privacy practices described on the store listing.
  • Gameplay progress and preferences typically stay on your device unless you use Apple system features that sync data according to your settings.
